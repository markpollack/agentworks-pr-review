### 2. `a607c59` (2026-08-13) — Migrate to agent-judge normalized judgments (**the Step 2.4D proof point**)
Adds `agent-judge-core:0.14.0-SNAPSHOT` to `dependencyManagement` in `pom.xml` and consumes the normalized API across 8 files: **net −31 lines**. Two concrete shapes:
- `Judgment.builder().score(new BooleanScore(x)).status(x ? PASS : FAIL)...` → **`Judgment.verdict(allPassed)`** (BuildJudge, QualityJudge, VersionPatternJudge) — the score/status redundancy collapses into one call.
- Metadata decoration by full rebuild → **`judgment.toBuilder().metadata(...)`** (BuildGate and the three DSL steps) — `toBuilder()` is the new affordance that made the copy-every-field pattern unnecessary.
- Also `Gate.evaluate` now returns **`GateAssessment`** (`GateAssessment.decided(GateDecision.PASS)`) rather than a bare `GateDecision` — a workflow-flows change riding along.
